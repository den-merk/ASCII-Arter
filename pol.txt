An example is currently shown there.

Enter the so-called palette here (into first line) - 
that is a gradient of characters,
where the first character corresponds to 
the fewest filled pixels and the last to the most.

It is also recommended to place a space at the very beginning, 
since it contains no filled pixels at all.
